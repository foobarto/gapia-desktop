# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Gapia Desktop contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation
welcoming, professional, and safe. Constructive disagreement about technical
decisions is welcome; personal attacks are not.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- giving specific, actionable, and respectful feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- respecting privacy, licensing boundaries, and responsible disclosure;
- keeping discussions relevant to the project; and
- considering the safety and accessibility impact of hardware-control changes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, sexualized content,
threats, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, or sustained
disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behavior conflicts with these
standards. Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, recurrence,
and the safety of affected participants.

Report conduct concerns privately to `bartosz@foobarto.me` with `[conduct]`
in the subject. Do not use a public issue. Reports will be handled with
discretion, but absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where action or
legal compliance requires disclosure.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project.
